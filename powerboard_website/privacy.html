Privacy Policy

PowerBoard for macOS · Last updated 6 August 2026

PowerBoard is built so your work stays yours. There are no accounts, no ads, and no third-party trackers. This page describes everything the app stores and everything that can leave your Mac.

Your boards stay with you

Boards, the images you place on them, and the files you export are stored on your Mac in PowerBoard's own application-support folder. If snapshot backups are on, compressed copies are written to your own iCloud Drive — which is your Apple account, not ours. We operate no server that holds your boards, and we cannot read them.

Agent connections are local, and you start them

PowerBoard can serve an MCP endpoint on your own machine (127.0.0.1) so an AI client you already run — Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, or another — can read and edit the board you are looking at. That endpoint is local to your Mac; PowerBoard does not send your boards anywhere on its own.

What the AI client you connect then does with the content it reads is governed by that client and its provider, under their own privacy terms. Connecting an agent is an explicit action you take, and you can stop serving MCP by quitting the app.

No analytics

The app collects no usage analytics, no telemetry, and no crash reports of its own, and it contains no advertising identifiers and no cross-app tracking of any kind.

Feedback

If you email us, we receive the message you send and the address you sent it from, and use them only to reply to you.

Supporting PowerBoard

Every feature is free. If you choose to contribute, the payment is processed by Stripe on Stripe's own site — we never see your card details, and no purchase is required for anything in the app.

What we never collect

No location, no contacts, no photos beyond the images you place on a board yourself, no microphone, no advertising identifiers.

Data retention and deletion

Boards live on your Mac and in your iCloud; deleting them there deletes them everywhere we know of. To have email correspondence deleted, write to the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the updated page will appear here with a new date before the change takes effect.
