Support

PowerBoard for macOS · Help & contact

PowerBoard is made by one person, and support is answered by the same person who writes the code.

Get in touch

Email danny@lamonade.xyz. Bug reports are most useful with your app version and, if you can, the board it happened on.

Common questions

  • Where are my boards? On your Mac, as JSON in PowerBoard's own application-support folder, with snapshot backups in your iCloud Drive. Nothing is stored on a server, and there is no account.
  • Something didn't save? PowerBoard surfaces failed writes visibly rather than swallowing them. If you see that state, tell us — a silent save failure is treated as the most serious class of bug in this app.
  • How do I connect an agent? Open Connect agent in the top right while a board is open. It gives you the MCP URL to paste into Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, or any other MCP client, along with the board id if you want to point an agent at one specific board.
  • An agent edit went wrong. Agent edits are ordinary board operations, so ⌘Z undoes them exactly like your own.
  • How do I get it? PowerBoard is free on the Mac App Store. It needs an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 12 or later.
  • Refunds? Contributions are optional, unlock nothing, and are processed by Stripe. If you contributed by mistake, email us and we'll refund it.
