Usage

Do

  • Keep ≥10% clear space around the tile (the built-in macOS margin).
  • Keep the node flat — depth comes from layered rings, not shading.
  • Use the tuned small assets below 64px (rings dropped).
  • Pair with one accent; charcoal or white ground only.

Don't

  • Add a second accent color or recolor the node.
  • Render the node as a glossy 3D sphere.
  • Keep both rings at 16–32px (they muddy).
  • Place the glyph on a busy or mid-tone photo.
